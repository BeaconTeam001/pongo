The Concept

Turning daycare dogs into cherished memories.

We bring professional school-style photography to shelters, daycares, and boarding facilities across Tampa Bay. Quick 2-3 minute sessions, SMS galleries within 24 hours, and packages pet parents actually want to buy.

This isn't just photography—it's a scalable business model with 60%+ conversion rates, recurring revenue, and genuine community impact.

Launch Strategy

Three-phase roadmap to $26K monthly revenue.

01

Give Back

Event-based portrait days tied to holidays, birthdays, adoption anniversaries, and seasonal moments.

02

Monetize

Launch paid portrait days at daycares and boarding facilities. SMS galleries, fast checkout, revenue sharing. Test holiday themes and upsells.

03

Scale

Build routes of 10-12 facilities with recurring monthly sessions. Automate galleries, payments, delivery. Introduce mugs, cards, canvas prints.
